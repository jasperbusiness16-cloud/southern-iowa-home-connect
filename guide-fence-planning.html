Planning a new fence in Iowa

A few decisions before estimates can make contractor conversations much faster.

Know the purpose

Privacy, pets, livestock, property definition and appearance call for different materials and heights.

Understand the property line

Do not assume an old fence marks the legal boundary. Surveys and local rules may matter.

Think about access

Plan gate locations and whether equipment or trailers need to pass.

Compare complete proposals

Ask whether posts, gates, removal, cleanup and warranty are included.
