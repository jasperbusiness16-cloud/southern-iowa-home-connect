Provider standards

Clear expectations before we call anyone a partner.

Planned onboarding rules that will be refined before provider listings become live.

Identity and contact information

Providers should supply a real business name, current contact details and an accountable business contact.

Licensing and insurance where applicable

We will not claim licensing or insurance unless it can be checked through suitable documentation or authoritative sources.

Service-area confirmation

Providers identify which counties, towns and categories they actually serve.

Customer conduct

Repeated serious complaints, deceptive practices or misuse of homeowner contact information may lead to removal.

Availability labels

“Accepting requests” or similar labels will only be displayed from provider-supplied status.

Paid placement

Featured placement, if offered later, will be labeled and will not be presented as an objective best-provider ranking.
