Referral service

Southern Iowa Home Connect is intended to operate as a connection/referral service. It does not perform home-improvement, repair, septic, roofing, plumbing or other contracting work itself.

Independent providers

Any professional or company introduced through the service will operate independently. Homeowners are responsible for deciding whether to hire a provider and should independently verify licensing, insurance, pricing and suitability where applicable.

No guarantee of availability

Submitting a future request will not guarantee that a provider is available, that a provider will accept the project or that any particular price or outcome will be offered.

The current website is in development. Request forms shown during this stage are previews and do not transmit information.
