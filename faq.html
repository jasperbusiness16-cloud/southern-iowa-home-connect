FAQ

How Home Connect is supposed to work.

Is it free for homeowners?

The planned homeowner matching service is free to request. Contractors may eventually pay for qualified opportunities, memberships or clearly labeled placement.

Do I have to hire anyone?

No. A match or introduction creates no obligation to hire, request an estimate or accept a price.

Does Home Connect perform the work?

No. Southern Iowa Home Connect is a referral and information service. Providers operate independently.

How many contractors may contact me?

The planned default is a small number of relevant providers—generally up to three when available—rather than blasting a request to a large list.

Who receives my information?

Once live, necessary request details may be shared with participating providers serving the requested category and area. The final privacy policy will identify the actual production data flow.

How are providers ranked?

We do not currently rank providers. Paid placement, if added, will be labeled. Ratings and availability will only be shown when sourced or confirmed.

What does verified mean?

We are not using a verified badge yet. Any future verification program will publish concrete requirements.

What counts as a qualified lead?

The planned definition includes a real person, valid contact details, relevant service need, an in-territory property and protection against obvious spam or duplicates.
