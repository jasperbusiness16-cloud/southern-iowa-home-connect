Why is my septic alarm going off?

A septic alarm often indicates liquid in a pump or holding chamber is higher than expected, but the exact cause needs checking.

Do not ignore it

Reduce unnecessary water use until you know what is happening.

Possible causes

Pump problems, float issues, electrical faults, high water use or groundwater conditions can contribute.

Avoid risky DIY work

Septic tanks can contain dangerous gases and unsanitary conditions. Never enter a tank or chamber.

Call for service when needed

If the alarm persists, wastewater backs up or other warning signs appear, contact a septic professional.

General homeowner information only. Conditions vary by property. For safety hazards, sewage, structural damage, electrical concerns or active leaks, contact an appropriate professional.
