Common septic needs

Start with the problem you’re having.

Septic pumping

Routine tank pumping or pumping needed because of slow drains, odors or a full system.

Backups & urgent issues

Wastewater backing up, sewage odors, standing water or other signs that need prompt attention.

Inspections

System checks related to home sales, maintenance planning or suspected problems.

Repairs

Tank, line, baffle, distribution or drain-field concerns that may require professional evaluation.

Alarms & pumps

High-water alarms, lift-pump issues or other components that are not operating normally.

Replacement questions

Help finding a professional when a system may need significant repair or replacement.

Know the signs

When it’s worth calling a septic professional.

01

Multiple drains are moving slowly at the same time.

02

You notice sewage odors indoors or near the tank/drain field.

03

There is standing water or unusually wet ground over the system area.

04

A septic alarm is sounding or wastewater is backing up.
