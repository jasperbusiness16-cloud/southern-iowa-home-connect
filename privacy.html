Pre-launch notice: The current request form is a non-submitting preview and does not transmit the information entered into it.

This page is a pre-launch placeholder and will be updated before Southern Iowa Home Connect begins collecting or sharing homeowner lead information.

Information we may collect

When the live service launches, a request may include your name, phone number, email address if requested, town or ZIP code, the service you need and details you voluntarily provide about the project.

How information may be used

Information submitted through a live request form may be used to respond to you, determine whether your request is within our service area and connect you with an independent service provider that may be able to help.

Provider sharing

Before the form goes live, this policy will explain when and how request information may be shared with participating independent providers.
