Privacy

Current status: Development forms do not transmit, store or share information entered into them.

Before live collection

This policy will be updated with the actual data processor, storage, retention, business contact and categories of providers that may receive request data.

Potential request data

Name, phone, email, town or ZIP, county, property relationship, service category, urgency, contact preference, project description and optional photos.

Purpose

Data may be used to determine service-area fit, match participating providers, prevent spam/duplicates, facilitate contact and measure lead quality.

Sharing

When live, necessary project details may be shared with a limited number of participating providers serving the requested category and location.

Analytics

The code is analytics-ready, but no production analytics identifier is configured yet.
