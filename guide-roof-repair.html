Roof leak or storm damage: what should you check first?

A safe check from the ground and inside can help you describe the issue without climbing onto a damaged roof.

Start inside

Look for dripping, ceiling stains, damp insulation or water near fixtures. Treat electrical proximity as a safety issue.

Check from the ground

Look for missing shingles, displaced metal, branches or debris without climbing onto the roof.

Document damage

Take dated photos of visible damage and interior water effects if safe to do so.

Temporary protection is not a repair

The location where water appears indoors may not be the roof entry point; a roofer can evaluate the exterior system.

General homeowner information only. Conditions vary by property. For safety hazards, sewage, structural damage, electrical concerns or active leaks, contact an appropriate professional.
